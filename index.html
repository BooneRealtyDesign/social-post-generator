1

Choose a clear photo of yourself, preferably from the shoulders up. You won't upload it to this page.

2

Click the blue button below. A new tab opens. If asked, sign in to ChatGPT.

3

Click the + or paperclip near the message box, select your photo, then click Open.

+→📷 Your headshot→Send ↑
4
Copy one pose prompt.

Click Copy prompt on a pose below. Go back to the ChatGPT tab, click the message box, press Ctrl + V (or Command + V on Mac), and send it.

5
Save the picture ChatGPT makes.

Open the finished image and choose its download option. Save it somewhere easy to find, such as your Downloads folder.

6
Come back here and upload it.

In the matching pose card, click Choose File, open your Downloads folder, select the saved image, and click Open. Repeat for each pose.

Important: Each pose prompt asks ChatGPT to keep your face recognizable, your proportions natural, and the image background transparent. Please check each image looks like you before uploading it.
Photo tip: Ask ChatGPT for a transparent PNG with your shoulders, arms and torso visible all the way to the bottom edge. The studio will place the bottom of your portrait directly against the contact-information bar, so you appear naturally grounded.
